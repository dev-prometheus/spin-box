# Landing page and interface copy

## Goal

Explain what users do, the money at risk, the possible outcomes and the next step. Use direct natural English. Adapt useful messaging hierarchy from the uploaded B2B copy references without importing SaaS trial/demo language into this consumer product.

## Page composition

Create a deliberate desktop hero with strong product imagery and substantial typography; compose a focused mobile version. Follow with how it works, outcome explanation, delivery versus liquidation, genuine evidence if available, FAQ, support and footer. Vary section composition; avoid one long stack of identical cards.

Use one clear primary action per task, with secondary help where useful. Do not show a zero-balance wallet as the defining action for signed-out visitors. Explain welcome interaction separately from paid play. A scripted welcome sequence is not evidence of random paid-game odds.

Before financial commitment show selected product/variant, verified value, stake, full-stake loss possibility, retry meaning and relevant fulfillment costs/conditions. Never describe winning as guaranteed. Do not frame retries or a near miss as proof the next attempt is likely to win.

## Language

Prefer Wallet, Deposit, Withdraw, Orders, Activity, Rewards and Referrals. Explain liquidation as taking the stated cash amount instead of delivery; distinguish wallet funding from external payout. Avoid Crypto Treasury, ledger names, RPCs and scraper jargon in customer journeys. Use sentence case and concise action verbs. Keep financial figures in one selected currency, with explicit conversion context when necessary.

Write errors with what happened, whether money moved, and a safe next action. A timeout is not proof of failure: show checking status before inviting a new financial submission. Preserve form input after recoverable failures.

## Truthfulness

Do not fabricate testimonials, delivery photos, winner feeds, customer counts, scarcity, affiliation, reserves or instant external payout claims. Use clearly labeled examples until real permissioned evidence exists. Keep shipping timing, withdrawal timing and fees accurate to implemented operations. Reward copy must use the confirmed formula and eligibility, not fixed historical examples.

## Deliverable checks

Read the page as a first-time visitor: can they identify the product, stake/risk, outcome paths and next action without opening legal text? Check mobile line breaks, large currency amounts and text expansion. Record any claim awaiting operational evidence. Do not publish placeholder claims as facts.
